# Ad Copy Bank

Voice rules (the Stone Systems lesson): write like you talk on a jobsite. Short
sentences. Real numbers. No "unlock", "elevate", "revolutionize", "game-changing",
"harness the power". Talk about the business, not the reader's identity (Meta
compliance). Every ad ends with one clear ask.

---

## Primary text variants

### Angle 1: The missed call math
**A1.**
A plumbing company misses about 30% of its inbound calls. Average job: $400 to $900.
Do that math for a month and it's a truck payment. Do it for a year and it's a truck.
We built a system that texts every missed call back in under 60 seconds, has the
conversation, and puts a callback on the calendar. Watch it happen in the video.

**A2.**
Somebody called a contractor today, got voicemail, and hired the next company on
Google. Took about 4 minutes. This 4 minute video shows the system that makes sure
that customer is yours instead: 5 second text-back, after-hours answering, and a
review machine. Watch the whole thing before your competitor does.

### Angle 2: Speed to lead
**A3.**
The first business to respond wins the job about 8 times out of 10. Most contractors
respond in hours. This system responds in 5 seconds, by text, with the customer's
name and their actual problem, then books the appointment. There's a video of it
doing exactly that. Watch the right side of the screen.

### Angle 3: The review gate
**A4.**
One 1-star review costs a service business jobs for years. Here's what smart companies
run instead: every finished job triggers one text. Happy customers land on Google.
Unhappy ones land on the owner's phone, privately, before they ever open Google.
Reviews go up. Bad ones stop existing. Watch it work in the video.

### Angle 4: The website
**A5.**
Most contractor websites are one page, built years ago, ranking for nothing. The good
ones have a real page for every service and every city they work: 20+ pages of Google
real estate working around the clock. We build those sites, and they come wired with
automations that answer, book, and follow up while the crew works. See one in the video.

### Angle 5: After hours
**A6.**
Pipes burst at 11:47 PM. Furnaces die on Christmas Eve. The companies that win those
jobs aren't awake, they just have a system that is. Watch what happens in this video
when an emergency call hits a sleeping company that has one.

### Angle 6: Blunt honesty (the Stone-style disqualifier)
**A7.**
Straight up: if a service business already answers every call, follows up with every
lead in 5 minutes, and asks every customer for a review, this system won't do much
for it. Never met that business, but it might exist. For everyone else, this 4 minute
video shows the leaks and the machine that plugs them.

**A8.**
This is not "AI magic". It's four boring things done instantly, every time: text
back missed calls, respond to form fills in 5 seconds, answer after hours, ask for
reviews. Boring, instant, every time is how service businesses grow. Video shows all
four running.

### Angle 7: The demo offer (your wedge)
**A9.**
Every marketing agency says "trust me". Here's a different deal: we build a working
demo with the company's name, trade, and cities on it before any money changes hands.
Click around it. Show the crew. If it's not obviously worth it, delete the email.
Watch the video to see what the demo looks like.

### Angle 8: Retargeting (warm audiences only)
**A10.**
Still thinking about it? Fair. Here's the shortest version: one missed water heater
job pays for this system for a year. The demo takes 3 minutes to click through. Book
the 15 minute call and we'll build one with your company's name on it.

**A11.**
You watched the video. You saw the text go out 5 seconds after the form. That wasn't
a special effect, it's just software doing its job. 15 minute call, we build your
version first. Calendar's below.

## Video creative scripts (15 to 30 seconds, captions on)

**V1: The money shot (screen record only)**
Text overlay 0-2s: "Someone just filled out this plumber's form"
2-8s: form autofills and submits
8-15s: phone lights up, text arrives (zoom on phone)
15-25s: customer replies, booking confirms
Overlay: "5 seconds. Booked. Nobody touched anything."
CTA: "Watch the full 4 minute demo"

**V2: Missed call (screen record only)**
Overlay 0-2s: "This plumber just missed a call. Watch."
Call screen → No Answer → "47 seconds later" tag on the text-back
Conversation to the 2:30 callback
Overlay: "Dead lead → callback on the calendar"

**V3: Talking head + cutaway**
You, phone camera, 0-4s: "If you run a service company, this is the most expensive
thing that happens to you every week." Cut to missed call demo. 20s. Back to you:
"Full demo's linked. Four minutes. Do the math after."

**V4: Review gate (screen record)**
Overlay: "Customer had a bad experience. Watch where the review goes."
2-star tap → private form → Owner Alert card on screen
Overlay: "Google never saw it. The owner did."
