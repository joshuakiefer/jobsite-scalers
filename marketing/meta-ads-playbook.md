# Meta Ads Playbook: Jobsite Scalers

Modeled on the Stone Systems approach (flat-fee contractor systems, blunt authentic
voice, volume creative testing) with your differentiators layered on: custom sites
instead of templates, and a clickable live demo nobody else has.

## Step 0: Study their ads properly (do this yourself, 90 minutes)

The Meta Ad Library can't be scraped from here, so this part is on you:

1. Go to facebook.com/ads/library, country US, search "Stone Systems" and also browse
   their Facebook page's ad tab.
2. For every RUNNING ad older than 30 days (longevity = it's working), record in a
   sheet: hook (first line / first 3 seconds), format (talking head, screen record,
   static), offer wording, CTA, landing page URL.
3. You're not stealing copy. You're extracting the skeleton: what pain they open with,
   how fast they show the product, how they price-anchor, what they ask you to do.
4. Repeat for 2 or 3 other contractor-marketing advertisers you respect. Patterns that
   appear across all of them are the market talking. Copy the patterns, not the words.

## The funnel

Ad → VSL landing page → book a call (GHL calendar) → sales call → close.

One extra weapon they don't have: after the VSL, link the live demo
("Click around the actual system") as a secondary CTA. A prospect who spends 3 minutes
pressing buttons on your demo is half closed before the call.

## Account setup (day 1)

- Business Manager + ad account + page. Verify the domain of your landing page.
- Pixel on the landing page, plus GHL's Meta integration (or CAPI via GHL) so the
  "booked a call" event flows back as the conversion. Optimize for the call booking,
  never for landing page views. If volume is thin early, optimize for a mid-funnel
  event (VSL 50% watch or calendar page view) until you get ~10 bookings/week, then
  switch to the booking event.
- Set up the GHL calendar with qualifying questions on the booking form: trade,
  years in business, roughly how many calls they miss a week. Disqualify tire kickers
  before they eat your calendar.

## Campaign structure (keep it stupid simple)

- 1 campaign, Sales objective, CBO OFF at first (use ABO so you control test spend).
- 3 ad sets to start, $20 to $30/day each ($60 to $90/day total):
  - Ad set A: broad, US, 30 to 60, no interests. Creative does the targeting.
  - Ad set B: interest stack: small business owners + home improvement/trade interests
    (plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrician, general contractor).
  - Ad set C: retargeting: VSL viewers 50%+, landing page visitors, demo clickers,
    page/IG engagers, 30 days.
- 3 to 5 ads per ad set, same ads across A and B. Let Meta pick winners.
- Do not touch anything for 5 to 7 days except killing an ad with CTR under 0.5%
  after ~2k impressions. Judge on cost per booked call, nothing else.

## Creative strategy (your unfair advantage)

Stone runs talking-head authenticity. You can run that AND something they can't:
**the demo itself is the creative.** Screen recordings of the money shot convert
because the product demos itself in 15 seconds.

Priority order:
1. **Demo clip ads (15 to 30s, 1:1 and 9:16).** The form-fill money shot with captions.
   Hook text on screen for the first 2 seconds: "Watch what happens 5 seconds after
   someone fills out this plumber's form." No voiceover needed, captions carry it.
2. **Talking head + demo cutaway.** You on camera for 5 seconds ("If you run a service
   business, watch this"), cut to the demo, back to you for the CTA. Phone camera,
   natural light, no teleprompter energy. This is the Stone format.
3. **Statics.** Screenshot of the owner alert ("2-star review intercepted, never
   reached Google") with headline overlay. Screenshot of the 47-seconds text-back.
   Screenshot of the stats card.
4. Later: client results screenshots, of course, once you have them.

Refresh cadence: 2 to 3 new creatives per week. Kill losers weekly. The winners will
die every 3 to 6 weeks; keep the pipeline moving.

## Benchmarks (service-business B2B on Meta, 2026-ish reality)

- CPM: $25 to $60 for this audience. Don't panic at $50.
- CTR (link): 1%+ is good, under 0.5% kill it.
- Cost per booked call: $40 to $150 is workable if your offer closes at $300 to $500/mo
  recurring. Judge nothing until you've spent at least 3x your target cost per call.
- Watch VSL retention in the ad account and on the landing page (Wistia/VTurb style
  player with retention graph). If people bail before the money shot, move it earlier.

## Compliance notes (Meta will slap you here)

- Never call out the reader's attributes: "Are you a plumber losing money?" can trip
  personal-attributes review. Safer: "Plumbing companies lose $X to missed calls."
  Speak about the business, not about "you" being something.
- No fake UI elements in statics (fake play buttons, fake notification counts on the
  ad image itself). Screenshots of your real demo UI are fine.
- Don't promise income ("add $10k/mo guaranteed"). Show mechanisms and math instead.

## Offer positioning (where you beat Stone Systems)

Their model: $297/mo, white-labeled GHL, template websites, honest "this is a
foundation, not magic" framing. It works. Respect it. Your wedge:

1. **Custom site, not a template.** Show their template next to your Summit demo.
   The line: "There are 4,000 contractors running the same template site. Yours
   shouldn't be one of them."
2. **20 SEO pages per client**, generated and live in week one.
3. **The live demo.** "I'll build a demo with your company name on it before you pay
   a dollar" is an offer almost nobody makes and your template generator makes it a
   30 minute task. That's your Stone-style honest hook: don't trust me, click it.

Price: anchor against their $297 however you want, but the demo-first offer supports
premium pricing ($400 to $600/mo or setup fee + monthly). Decide after 10 sales calls,
not before.

## Week one checklist

- [ ] Landing page live: VSL + calendar embed + demo link, pixel firing
- [ ] GHL calendar with qualifying questions
- [ ] 5 creatives: 2 demo clips, 1 talking head, 2 statics
- [ ] Campaign live at $60 to $90/day
- [ ] Ad Library research sheet done (Stone + 2 competitors)
- [ ] Answer every booked call within 5 minutes with... your own speed-to-lead. Eat
      your own cooking; mention it on the sales call.
